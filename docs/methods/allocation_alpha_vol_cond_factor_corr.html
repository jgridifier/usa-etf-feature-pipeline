2. What the original work found

DeMiguel, Martín-Utrera & Uppal (2024), Journal of Finance — lead citation

  • Setup: Academic equity factors; compare (i) unconditional multifactor mean–variance, (ii) single-factor volatility-managed overlays in the Moreira–Muir spirit, and (iii) a conditional multifactor portfolio whose factor weights respond to market volatility (conditional mean / covariance of factor returns).
  • Claims:
    • Single-factor vol-managed strategies that look strong in-sample often fail OOS (Cederburg et al.) and/or net of costs (Barroso & Detzel).
    • A conditional multifactor portfolio can outperform its unconditional multifactor counterpart OOS and after costs (authors report ~13% higher OOS Sharpe of net returns in their primary table, difference significant).
    • Factor risk prices generally decrease as market volatility rises — so cutting factor exposure in high-vol states is economically coherent in a multifactor pricing sense.
  • Result shape: Gains attributed to conditioning the multifactor mean–variance solution on market vol, not to a static risk-parity or a single scalar leverage clock alone.
  • Limitations: Academic factors and long histories; estimation of conditional μ / Σ is nontrivial; ETF implementation faces turnover, cash constraints, and thinner category sleeves than published factor libraries. We do not claim their OOS Sharpe transfers to our USA ETF panel.

Moreira & Muir (2017), Journal of Finance — unconditional backbone (already Book 2)

  • Setup / claim: Scale factor exposure by inverse recent variance; Sharpe / alpha improvements vs unmanaged versions when expected returns do not offset vol spikes.
  • Our status: Implemented as Book-2 Option A vol-target (scale-down into BIL). Empirical on our short common window: risk improvement yes; return-edge NW t ≈ 0 — hence this conditional upgrade.

Harvey et al. (2018), Journal of Portfolio Management

  • Practitioner target-vol: constant-vol scaling across assets; equity risk-assets drive Sharpe gains; left-tail often milder when high-vol states coincide with cut exposure.

Bailey & López de Prado (2014) — mandatory inference

  • Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) / trial-count adjustment on every OOS claim once we grid lookbacks, correlation estimators, or gates.

3. Core math / intuition

Unconditional Book-2 (already live) — target-vol form:

σ̂_t = √252 · stdev( last L daily returns of r^A ) # data ≤ t f_t = clip( σ* / σ̂_t , f_min, f_max ) # primary: f_max=1 (scale-down only) w_t = f_t · w^A + (1 − f_t) · e_cash # cash = BIL

Paper-style conditional multifactor sketch (research mapping — implementable approximation on ETF category sleeves, not a WRDS factor dump):

Let F_t be a K-vector of category-sleeve (or factor-proxy) excess returns. Conditioning variable: z_t = market vol (or 1/σ̂_mkt,t), estimated ≤ t. Conditional moments (expanding / rolling, data ≤ t only): μ̂_t(z) , Σ̂_t(z) Conditional mean–variance weights (long-only / cash residual in v1): w*_t ∝ Σ̂_t(z)^{-1} μ̂_t(z) then scale or clip to sum ≤ 1; residual → BIL Alternative (lighter Eng path — CoS-friendly): keep Book-2 f_t from portfolio vol, multiply by a correlation gate g_t ∈ [g_min, 1]: g_t = 1 when mean pairwise |corr| of sleeves is “calm” g_t → g_min when conditional correlations spike with high z_t effective equity scale: f̃_t = f_t · g_t

Intuition in one sentence: Unconditional vol-target cuts risk when the ride is bumpy; the paper says you also need the multifactor / correlation state — factor risk prices and sleeve co-movement change with market vol — so gate or re-budget exposures conditionally, not with a single scalar alone.

What we are not doing: rebuilding Spectral RP or Regime-Aware round-1; launching a new 3-ETF toy book; claiming DeMiguel OOS Sharpe on our panel; production trading or WRDS proprietary factors.

4. What we will do on USA ETF / growth-panel data

Assumptions

  • Universe gate: only tickers in usa_universe_categorized.csv; Appendix 3 denied.
  • Primary path: Book-2 upgrade — gate/rescale existing vol-target / multifactor category sleeves using conditional correlations (or inverse market vol as z_t per paper), on N≥100 liquid names and/or Category aggregates.
  • Optional robustness: same gate on growth-panel Option A + BIL (parity with live Book-2 artifacts).
  • Monthly rebalance; decision at month-end t uses prices/returns/corr ≤ t; performance on month t+1 only (no same-month leakage).
  • Costs: 5 bps one-way on turnover.
  • Sharpe_rf0 (rf=0) for memo parity; NW t and DSR mandatory.
  • Thin-history flags (thin_lt5y and shorter) published with/without thin names.

Inputs

  • Universe / categories: /workspace/investments/usa_universe_categorized.csv
  • USA panels: usa_universe_panel_monthly_returns.csv, usa_universe_adj_close.csv, coverage CSVs
  • Growth / Option A path (Book-2 parity): growth_alpha_adj_close.csv, growth_panel_monthly_returns.csv, vol_target_real_bil/*
  • Existing CLI / module: walkforward-vol-target in jgridifier/usa-etf-feature-pipeline

Walk-forward / leakage rules

  • σ̂_t, Σ̂_t, μ̂_t, corr gates, and any z_t at month-end t must be invariant to mutating returns after t (unit test).
  • No same-month macro prints or same-month return labels in features.
  • Register every trial (L, z-rule, corr-estimator, g_min, sleeve-set, apply-to) for DSR / trial count — same spirit as wf_strategy_registry.csv / vol_target_trial_registry.csv.

Nulls

  • (a) Unconditional Moreira–Muir / current Book-2 vol-target Option A (primary null — must beat or clearly improve risk-adjusted path after costs + DSR).
  • (b) Static Option A (VOO 70 / QQQM 20 / IJR 10).
  • (c) Optional: equal-weight category sleeves (unconditional).

5. How we will know it worked

Success (research bar — not a trade signal):

  1. OOS walk-forward equity curve + metrics vs nulls (a)–(c) exist and are reproducible from a CLI in usa-etf-feature-pipeline.
  2. Primary config shows either (a) higher Sharpe_rf0 with NW t meaningfully positive vs Book-2 unconditional vol-target after costs, or (b) clearly better MaxDD / left-tail with only modest Sharpe give-up — Jared / CoS choose which bar binds.
  3. DSR + trial count reported; leakage unit tests green; 5 bps costs applied.
  4. Diagnostics: occupancy of high-corr / high-z states; avg next-month active return when gate is binding vs open.
  5. Does not silently win by reintroducing score-based name picking or peeking same-month labels.

Failure is informative: If the conditional gate adds nothing vs Book-2 vol-target after DSR, we keep unconditional Option A vol-target as the live Book-2 default and archive this extension with a one-line null result — same honesty as Justina round-1.
