Trial-13 DSR feasibility check

Result: trial 13 can’t pass C4. C4 needs DSR ≥ 0.95. With 13 trials over 119 months, that takes a Sharpe ex-BIL of about 1.24, versus 0.645 for capped equal weight over the same months, about 1.9 times as much. With fat tails the hurdle rises to 1.30 to 1.37. The hurdle is a lower bound. If trial 13 scores well, it raises the cross-trial variance, and that raises SR0.

Registry-only arithmetic · no gate run, no new trial, no signal on the panel looked at · not an archived trial · research on an experimental ETF panel, not investment advice. Nothing here changes Book 1, Book 2 or the vol-target backbone.
