Spectral Risk Parity

Research archive — not a promoted method. Walk-forward OOS did not beat Ledoit–Wolf MinVar (name) and lagged all nulls (sleeve). Teaching note + OOS tables kept for documentation; no book map / showcase.

Experimental research only; not investment advice. ADIA Lab / Agnostic Risk Parity teaching note and citations · Methods

This implementation maps the teaching sketch to correlation PCs with a unit-variance MP edge. Signal exposures are inverse square-root eigenvalue weighted. A nonnegative quadratic projection penalizes noise exposures; inverse volatility converts these exposures to capital weights. This is a research mapping, not an exact ADIA replication.

Weights decided at month-end use only prior and current observations. The next complete month earns returns, less 5 bps times half the absolute target-weight change (initial turnover 0.5). Category sleeves use equal ETF weights internally. Missing next-month observations raise an error.

Snapshot membership, thin-history flags and ADV are not historical point-in-time metadata; survivorship and selection bias remain. A separate expanding 60-observation gate excludes thin names at each decision. The default ADV filter is off; enabling it can cause the 100-name gate to fail. Partial final source months are excluded.
