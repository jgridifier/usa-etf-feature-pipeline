1. Why this paper / blog

The Aug 2026 Justina newsletter highlighted Spectral Risk Parity from the ADIA Lab research series (López de Prado, Lipton & Balasubramanian, Feb 2026). For a ~377-name USA ETF panel with heavy Defined Outcome / Specialty sleeves, classical equal-risk-contribution (ERC) and sample-covariance Markowitz both degrade: eigenvalues are noisy, and “risk parity” on raw assets can silently load noisy eigenmodes.

Why it fits this panel: Spectral RP uses random-matrix / Marčenko–Pastur structure to separate informative principal components from noise, then builds a risk-parity portfolio that tracks the PC portfolio while penalizing noise exposure (López de Prado, Lipton & Balasubramanian, 2026, ADIA Lab). That scales to ≥100 liquid names and to category sleeves without being a growth-25 toy.

Related eigenrisk literature (Benichou et al., 2016; Bernardi, Leippold & Lohre, 2019) shows that equalizing risk across eigenvectors is more robust to estimation error than asset-level ERC. Spectral RP is the CIO shortlist pick that makes that idea operational with an explicit noise penalty.

2. What the original work found

López de Prado, Lipton & Balasubramanian (2026), ADIA Lab Research Paper Series

  • Setup: Construct risk-parity portfolios in the eigenbasis of the covariance/correlation matrix; identify signal vs noise eigenvalues via the Marčenko–Pastur (MP) law from random matrix theory (RMT).
  • Claim: A spectral portfolio that tracks the principal-component portfolio while explicitly penalizing exposure to noisy components is robust to noise by construction and outperforms equal-risk-contribution (ERC) and minimum-variance (MinVar) empirically (López de Prado, Lipton & Balasubramanian, 2026).
  • Economic intuition: Most sample eigenvalues of a high-N / moderate-T correlation matrix are sampling noise. Allocating “equal risk” on raw assets can still concentrate on noisy modes. Penalizing those modes is a structural fix, not a heuristic shrinkage knob.
  • Limitations (as framed for our test): Full empirical tables / asset universe of the ADIA note were not re-run here; MP edge depends on aspect ratio \(q = N/T\); long-only ETF constraints and turnover costs are ours to add. Cite ADIA Lab page for the published abstract claims; treat OOS numbers as to-be-replicated, not gospel.

Supporting eigenrisk / RMT context

  • Benichou et al. (2016), arXiv:1610.08818 — Agnostic / Eigenrisk Parity: Equal realized risk on all principal components; “agnostic” form \(\boldsymbol{\pi}^* \propto \mathbf{C}_{\mathrm{RIE}}^{-1/2}\mathbf{p}\) when indicator correlations are unknown. Motivates eigenbasis risk budgeting.
  • Bernardi, Leippold & Lohre (2019), Journal of Risk: Principal risk parity is immune to second-order (estimation) risk among alternative RP strategies; eigenvalue adjustment mitigates SOR bias for others.
  • López de Prado (2016), Journal of Portfolio Management — HRP: Related ADIA-lineage risk-parity family using hierarchy rather than spectral noise penalties; useful null / sibling, not the same method.

3. Core math / intuition

Sample correlation / covariance and eigendecomposition (at month-end \(t\), using only returns ≤ \(t\)):

Σ̂_t = cov({r_{t-L+1}, …, r_t}) or correlation R̂_t R̂_t = U Λ Uᵀ , Λ = diag(λ₁ ≥ … ≥ λ_N)

Marčenko–Pastur noise bulk (for aspect ratio \(q = N/T\), variance σ² of residuals; classic RMT — Marčenko & Pastur, 1967; applied to finance in Laloux et al. / Bun–Bouchaud–Potters cleaning literature):

λ ∈ [λ₋, λ₊] treated as noise bulk λ₊ = σ² (1 + √q)² (upper MP edge; use cleaned / fitted σ² in practice) Signal set S = { k : λ_k > λ₊ } (plus optional market mode handling) Noise set N = { k : λ_k ≤ λ₊ }

Spectral Risk Parity intuition (López de Prado, Lipton & Balasubramanian, 2026):

1) Form PC / eigen-risk-parity weights on signal modes (equal risk per informative PC). 2) Explicitly penalize exposures that load on noise modes N. 3) Map back to asset (or sleeve) weights; long-only + sum-to-1 for ETFs. 4) Compare to ERC (asset-level equal risk contribution) and MinVar.

Implementable research sketch (panel mapping — not a claim that this is the ADIA closed form verbatim):

w_signal ∝ U_S · (equal-risk-on-modes in S) w ← proj_longonly( w_signal − γ · load_on_noise(U_N) ) normalize 1ᵀw = 1, w ≥ 0

Intuition in one sentence: pay for risk that lives in the informative spectrum; refuse to fund the Marčenko–Pastur junk pile.

What we are not doing in v1: full RIE/QuEST nonlinear shrinkage as the primary path; black-box ML; growth-25-only toys; claiming ADIA’s exact proprietary solver without the paper’s equations in hand.

4. What we will do on the USA ETF / category panel

Assumptions

  • Universe gate: only tickers in usa_universe_categorized.csv (~377). Returns panel: usa_universe_panel_monthly_returns.csv (~339 series). Coverage / thin_lt5y: usa_universe_panel_history_coverage.csv (117 of 339 flagged thin <5y).
  • Primary implementable universe: ≥100 liquid names with thin_lt5y=False and sufficient ADV proxy (research default: ADV ≥ ~$10M → ~125 names as of panel pull) and/or category sleeves (EW or ERC within category, then spectral RP across sleeves).
  • Defined Outcome / Buffer / Structured (139 names) and Specialty / Other (76): default exclude from primary spectral cov or sleeve-aggregate only; always carry thin_lt5y flags in outputs.
  • Monthly walk-forward; decision at month-end \(t\) uses returns ≤ \(t\); P&L on month \(t+1\) only — no same-month leakage.
  • Costs: ~5 bps one-way on monthly turnover (parity with existing WF memos). Sharpe_rf0 for cross-memo parity; also report excess vs cash sleeve.
  • Research framing only — not advice.

Inputs

  • Monthly returns: /workspace/investments/usa_universe_panel_monthly_returns.csv
  • Adj closes (if daily cov / MP edges needed): usa_universe_adj_close.csv
  • Categories: usa_universe_categorized.csv
  • History flags: usa_universe_panel_history_coverage.csv (thin_lt5y, years_monthly, adv_proxy)

Walk-forward / leakage rules

  • Lookback \(L\) months for Σ̂/R̂ at \(t\) uses only months ≤ \(t\) (primary grid: 36 / 60 / 84).
  • MP edge / noise cut fitted inside the lookback only; never peek \(t+1\).
  • Eligible set at \(t\): names with enough history inside lookback + not thin unless sleeve-level.
  • Register every trial (L, MP-cut rule, γ noise penalty, long-only, sleeve vs name) in strategy registry for DSR / multiplicity.

Nulls

  • Primary null: Static ERC on the same eligible set / same rebalance calendar (Maillard–Roncalli–Teiletche-style ERC; Roncalli, 2013).
  • Secondary null: Static long-only MinVar (Ledoit–Wolf cov) and equal-weight.
  • Optional sibling: Hierarchical Risk Parity (López de Prado, 2016) — same panel, same costs — to show spectral vs hierarchical diversification.
  • H0: Spectral RP does not improve OOS Sharpe_rf0 / MaxDD vs ERC after costs once DSR adjusts for the trial grid.

5. How we will know it worked

Success (research bar — not a trade signal):

  1. OOS walk-forward equity curve + metrics table vs ERC / MinVar / EW exist and are reproducible from a CLI in jgridifier/usa-etf-feature-pipeline.
  2. Primary config (≥100 liquid names or category-sleeve spectral RP) shows either (a) higher Sharpe_rf0 with Newey–West t meaningfully positive vs ERC, or (b) clearly better MaxDD / left-tail with modest Sharpe give-up — Jared chooses which bar binds.
  3. Raw Sharpe_rf0 AND Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) reported for the selected config. DSR mandatory: Bailey & López de Prado (2014), Journal of Portfolio Management — corrects selection bias under multiple testing and non-normality. Use strategy-registry trial count \(N\) and variance of trial Sharpes; optionally unified search-adjusted DSR if Eng wires the Aug 2026 newsletter variant. Do not ship a “win” on raw Sharpe alone.
  4. Leakage unit tests pass; costs applied; thin_lt5y flags in every OOS table.
  5. Eigen diagnostics: fraction of variance in signal modes; average noise-penalty contribution — shows the method used the spectrum, not a silent ERC clone.

Failure is informative: If spectral RP fails to beat ERC OOS after DSR, document that MP noise penalties did not add skill on this ETF panel (plausible if N is large but T is short, or if sleeves are already low-rank).
