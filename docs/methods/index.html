Ledoit & Wolf 2020 analytical NLS · mixed stock-and-bond panel · weekly 156w primary / 260w sensitivity · 65m OOS (2021-04 → 2026-08) · 5 bps

VOID — cash-dominated, no evidence of estimator edge. All criteria passed mechanically, but the test design was broken: the primary null (weekly LW MinVar) was ~68% in the cash-like category and the method ~81%, and Sharpe_rf0 rewards whichever holds more T-bills. NW t vs the null was +0.48 (156w) / +0.76 (260w): no evidence of a return edge. DSR at a low trial_count is effectively PSR vs zero and carries no weight.

Binding null: Weekly LW MinVar on the same names (primary)

No Sharpe table for this VOID run; see the verdict and the OOS artifact.

Quant cash-share caveat (2026-09-26): The 'US Treasuries / Govt / Cash-like' category used for the cash shares also contains duration (e.g. GOVT, IEF, TLT, SHY), while USFR sits in 'High Yield Credit'; quoted cash shares therefore mix in some duration and are not a pure T-bill share.

Bet 1 NLS GMV v2 (ex-cash nonlinear shrinkage min-variance)

Ledoit & Wolf 2020 analytical NLS · mixed panel, cash_like names excluded · weekly 156w primary / 260w sensitivity · 65m OOS (2021-04 → 2026-08) · 5 bps

VOID: short-duration dominated (pre-registered tripwire) — Quant, 2026-09-26. Pre-registered criterion 5 tripwire: at 156w the method averages 84.1% short_duration weight (SHY 57.8%, SPTS 26.1%) with effective N 2.21, and the primary null (weekly LW MinVar) 67.1% short_duration; the 260w run trips the same three. Short_duration plus FTSL is 96.58% of the 156w method book (84.12% + 12.46%, average over the 65 committed rebalances); FTSL is not re-scored. Even without the tripwire it would FAIL: excess-of-BIL Sharpe −0.49 vs the null's −0.18 (and below ERC's 0.33), and 260w points the same way (−0.53 vs −0.30).

Binding null: Weekly LW MinVar on the same names (primary)

No Sharpe table for this VOID run; see the verdict and the OOS artifact.

Quant estimator note (2026-09-26): The estimator did its job: it cut OOS vol by about a quarter vs linear LW (1.87% vs 2.48% at 156w, 1.89% vs 2.44% at 260w; LW2011 p < 0.001 in both windows), consistent with Ledoit & Wolf (2017). The failure comes from the min-variance objective on a mixed stock-and-bond universe, which concentrates in the lowest-vol bonds.

NW t: −1.14 vs weekly LW MinVar (156w); −0.67 (260w) · DSR (legacy rf = 0 Sharpe, vs zero): 0.018 on Sharpe ex-BIL (156w); not decisive
