Schur allocator gate results

Gate verdict: FAIL / ARCHIVE (Quant recompute confirmed). Quant recomputed the run from the committed outputs and confirmed the label: not book-eligible, no follow-up run, trial count stays 12. The Schur allocator failed 3 of 4 pre-registered criteria (C1, C3 and C4). Its Sharpe ex-BIL of 0.63 was above the primary null, LW MinVar (capped QP) (0.51), but not by a statistically significant margin (one-sided p = 0.132 HAC, 0.084 bootstrap). It edged HRP (Schur γ=0) by 0.01, which is within noise. It trailed Equal weight (capped) (0.65). Its deflated Sharpe ratio (0.433) is below the 0.95 bar once all 12 trials are counted. Tripwires: pass (effective N mean 22.9, cash-like share 0%). Not book-eligible. No book changes. What we learned: shrinking HRP toward MinVar barely moved the result, plain capped equal weight beat both, and the shallower drawdown than Book 1 came with a much lower Sharpe than the backbone (0.57 vs 0.86). Caveat: 68 months can only detect a Sharpe gap of roughly 0.7 vs Backbone; drawdown vs Book 1: single path, no test.
