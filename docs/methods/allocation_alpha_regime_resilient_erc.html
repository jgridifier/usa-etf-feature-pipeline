2. What the original work found

Ielpo, Muhammetgulyyeva & Royer (2026), Journal of Portfolio Management — lead citation

  • Title: “Building a Regime-Resilient ERC Portfolio.”
  • Authors / venue: Florian Ielpo, Selbi Muhammetgulyyeva, Julien Royer (LOIM / multi-asset research). Journal of Portfolio Management 52 (9): 189–213 (August 2026; online ~June 2026). DOI: 10.3905/jpm.2026.030.
  • Practitioner precursor: LOIM MARS note, Dec 2023 — “Building a regime-resistant, long-run allocation in multi asset” (same authors / regime-parity construction).
  • Setup (construction): Connect multi-regime return distributions with classical ERC. For each historical regime pool m, estimate a regime-conditional covariance and solve a local ERC; form the regime-parity portfolio as the probability-weighted sum of those local ERCs (πm = steady-state / historical frequencies). That is a change to how long-run ERC weights are built — not “pick today’s regime and hold only that portfolio.”
  • Motivation: Unconditional moments of a mixture crush regime structure; unconditional ERC can load excessively on hedging assets when rare stress regimes distort Σ. Regime parity restores linear-in-probability blending and claimed better resistance to rare adverse regimes / structural breaks.
  • Authors’ reported shape (paper / MARS — not our panel): Analytic two-asset Markov example shows unconditional ERC over-reacts to rare stress vs linear regime parity; multi-country equity/govvie/IG applications with expansion–recession dating; gains attributed mostly to regime-specific risks rather than correlation shifts; sensitivity to regime mis-identification (false discovery kills the edge).
  • Limitations for our test: Multi-asset (few names per geography), not a 100+ USA ETF panel; regime dating quality is the binding dependency. We do not claim their outperformance transfers. Our v1: USA panel / category sleeves, monthly WF, 5 bps, DSR + trial_count, primary null = unconditional ERC.

Maillard, Roncalli & Teiletche (2010) — classical ERC

  • “The Properties of Equally Weighted Risk Contribution Portfolios.” Journal of Portfolio Management 36 (4): 60–70. Backbone: equalize Euler risk contributions RCi(w) = wi (Σw)i so RCi = RCj for all i,j. Unconditional ERC is our primary null.

Diversification Under Stress / corr breakdown — construction overlays

  • CIO shortlist framing (Jun / Aug 2026): stress / correlation-breakdown overlays that change how ERC is built (e.g. mix unconditional Σ̂ with stress-window Σ̂; soft risk-budget caps when avg-|corr| / sync breaks down). These are first-class construction paths alongside LOIM regime-parity — not a discrete regime picker. Register all knobs for DSR.

Bailey & López de Prado (2014) — mandatory inference

  • Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) / trial-count adjustment on every OOS Sharpe claim once we grid regime rules, lookbacks, sleeve cuts, or stress overlays.

3. Core math / intuition

Classical ERC (Maillard–Roncalli–Teiletche): given Σ, find w ≥ 0, 1′w = 1, such that risk contributions are equal:

RC_i(w) = w_i (Σ w)_i , σ_p² = w′ Σ w = Σ_i RC_i(w) ERC: RC_i(w) = RC_j(w) ∀ i,j (equiv. minimize Σ_{i,j} (RC_i − RC_j)² or solve via iterative / QP forms)

Path B — Regime-parity construction (LOIM): regimes m = 1…M used as historical pools for local Σ̂ (not as a current-regime selector). Steady-state / historical frequencies πm (Σm πm = 1):

For each regime m (using returns dated in m, ≤ t only): Σ̂_m,t ← cov estimator on regime-m sample w*_m,t ← ERC(Σ̂_m,t) # long-only; residual → BIL if needed Regime-parity portfolio: w*_t = Σ_m π̂_m,t · w*_m,t If regimes are uninformative (Σ̂_m identical), w*_t ≡ unconditional ERC. π̂_m,t from historical frequencies or Markov steady-state estimated ≤ t.

Path A — Stress / corr-breakdown overlays (change ERC inputs):

Diagnostics (≤ t): stress-window Σ̂_stress, avg pairwise |corr|, sync / corr-breakdown score Σ̂_resilient,t = (1 − λ) Σ̂_uncond,t + λ Σ̂_stress,t # registered λ w*_t ← ERC(Σ̂_resilient,t) # still ERC — only Σ changed # Optional soft budget / cash residual when corr-breakdown elevates (registered)

Intuition in one sentence: Keep the ERC solver; make the risk inputs regime-resilient (stress-mixed Σ̂ and/or π-blend of local ERCs) — do not switch to a current-regime selected portfolio.

What we are not doing: dual-regime selection (Luo/Mulvey-style classify current regime → allocate); reopening Spectral RP / Regime-Aware dual / vol-cond-factor-corr / FT-MED; modifying live books (static core + Book-2 VT stay as-is); claiming LOIM OOS numbers on our panel; Forma; production trading or WRDS dumps; building #6 skewness on this ticket.

  1. OOS walk-forward equity curve + metrics vs nulls (a)–(c) [and optional (d)] exist and are reproducible from a CLI in usa-etf-feature-pipeline.
